# Media releases

The Media releases tab keeps a list of links to articles and posts, each matched to a brand.

## Adding links

Paste one or more links (up to 20 at a time) and click Add links. For each one the app reads the page on your computer, then scores every active brand:

- the brand name in the headline counts most
- the brand name in the page text, and in the link itself, counts less
- the link being on one of the brand's sites (set on the Brands tab) counts a lot, and so does a mention of one of its sites

A brand is assigned automatically only when it clearly stands out (a score of at least 2, ahead of every other brand). Otherwise the link stays Unassigned and shows "Possible: ..." so you can pick the brand yourself. You can change any match from the Brand dropdown; changes you make are never overwritten. After adding a new brand, click "Re-check unassigned" to match older links to it.

Tracking parts of links (utm_ and similar) are removed, so the same article is not saved twice.

## Limits

- **Some pages can't be read.** Posts on social sites often need a login, some sites block automatic reading, and PDFs and script-only pages have no text. Those links are still saved, labelled as unreadable, and matched from the link text only. Assign them by hand if needed.
- **Names that are common words can match by accident.** Names of three characters or fewer must match capital letters exactly. Check matches for brands with everyday names.
- **Only public web addresses are fetched.** Links to private networks, other ports, or addresses with logins are refused, including after redirects.
- In demo mode, links are kept in memory only and disappear when the demo ends.
